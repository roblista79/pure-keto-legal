Pure Keto Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

The short version
  • We collect what you enter to run the app: your account, your profile, and your food and health logs.
  • AI features send only the specific inputs listed in section 5 to Anthropic. We never send your email, password, weight, height, age, Apple Health data, or blood work.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app.

1. Who we are

Pure Keto is a mobile app for tracking keto nutrition, with AI tools for scanning recipes and foods, planning meals, and coaching. In this policy, "we", "us", and "our" mean the Pure Keto team. Contact us at privacy@ketocoach.app.

2. What we collect

What you give us

  • Account: your email address, your password (stored only as a secure hash), and the display name you choose.
  • Profile: details you enter during setup or in Settings, such as age, sex, height, weight, activity level, goals, dietary restrictions, and your country and city. You can also choose to tell us if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Logs: meals, macros, water, electrolytes, ketone readings, glucose readings, blood work entries, exercise, fasting, weight entries, streaks, saved recipes, shopping lists, and your conversations with the Coach.
  • Photos: pictures of recipes, meals, or food that you take or upload for scanning.
  • Barcodes: the barcode numbers you scan.

What we collect automatically

  • Usage analytics: which screens and features you use, such as scans, logged meals, and paywall views. Each event is stored with your account ID, your subscription tier, your country and city from your profile, your device type, your operating system, and your app version. It is stored in our own database. We do not use a third party analytics or advertising service.
  • Purchases: your subscription tier and token balance. Apple processes the payment. We never see your card or payment details.

4. Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, and only for the categories you approve, Pure Keto reads your weight, water intake, step count, and workouts. We use these to show your progress and keep your coaching accurate, and we treat imported values like anything else you enter yourself.

  • We do not use Apple Health data for advertising or marketing.
  • We do not sell it or share it with data brokers or advertisers.
  • We do not send it to AI providers.
  • You can turn access off any time in iPhone Settings, then Health, then Data Access and Devices.

5. How AI features use your data

Pure Keto uses Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, to identify foods, read recipes, build meal plans, and power the Coach. Each feature sends only what it needs:

FeatureWhat is sent to Anthropic
Recipe scan, Keto or Not, barcode lookupThe photo, the recipe text or ingredients, or the barcode number.
Meal plans and meal swapsYour dietary restrictions, daily macro targets, servings, cooking skill, and the meal being swapped.
Cheat Day reset planWhat you ate, how much, your current streak, and your daily carb limit.
CoachYour recent messages, your display name, your day count and phase, your streak, today's macros, hydration and electrolytes, your fasting status, your pregnancy or breastfeeding status if you set one, and short summaries of your past Coach conversations.

Never sent to Anthropic: your email, password, weight, height, age, Apple Health data, blood work entries, or location.

  • Under Anthropic's commercial API terms, inputs sent through the API are not used to train its models.
  • Food and barcode lookup results may be saved in shared caches on our servers to make the app faster. Those caches are not tied to your account.
  • We do not keep your photos. They are sent for analysis and are not saved to our storage.
  • AI output can be wrong. Pure Keto is not medical advice and does not replace your doctor, especially if you take medication, manage diabetes, are pregnant, or have a medical condition.

7. Affiliate links

Some shopping links in the app go to retailers such as Amazon or iHerb. If you buy after tapping one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. The retailer handles your purchase, your payment details, and your data under its own policy.

8. How long we keep data, and deleting it

We keep your data while your account exists. To delete it, open the Me tab, tap Delete Account, and type DELETE to confirm. This permanently deletes your account, your logs, your Coach conversations, your saved recipes, your token balance, and your usage history from our database. It cannot be undone.

Apple bills subscriptions, so deleting your account does not cancel one. Cancel it in your iPhone Settings under your Apple Account, then Subscriptions.

If you cannot use the in-app option, email privacy@ketocoach.app from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Thailand's PDPA, and California's CCPA give you rights over your data. In plain terms, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your data, in a machine readable format where possible
  • correct data that is wrong (most of it you can edit in the app)
  • delete your data
  • stop using your data for analytics linked to your account

Email privacy@ketocoach.app and we will reply within 30 days. If you are in the EU, UK, or Thailand, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Security

Data travels over HTTPS and is encrypted at rest by Supabase. Row level security rules mean each account can only read its own records. Your sign in token is kept in your device's secure storage. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

11. International transfers

Our providers may process your data in countries other than your own, including the United States. By using the app you understand your data may be handled there.

12. Children

Pure Keto is not for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

If we make a meaningful change, we will update the date above and tell you in the app. The current version is always at this address.

14. Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@ketocoach.app
